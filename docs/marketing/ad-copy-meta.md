# ResumeTailor — Meta Ad Copy (Instagram + Facebook)

> 12 ad variations. Each covers a segment/angle and is provided in **EN**, **pt-BR**, and **ES**.
> Each ad: Primary text · Headline · Description · CTA.
> Pricing tokens: **$9** (EN) / **R$39** (pt-BR) / use local "sin suscripción" framing (ES — price varies by country, keep "pago único" / "desde $9").
>
> **Compliance:** No hiring guarantees, no fabricated stats. "Match Score" is described as a tool output the user sees on their own document, never as a promise of being hired. Keep emoji light; Meta penalizes spammy formatting.

Recommended CTAs: **Apply Now**, **Sign Up**, **Get Offer**, **Learn More**. (Meta CTA buttons are picked from a fixed list — noted per ad.)

### Usage notes
- **Test matrix:** start with Ads 1, 2, 3 (volume + differentiator + proof) per market. Add segment-specific (4, 5, 6) once the angle winner is clear.
- **Creative pairing:** Ad 3 and Ad 12 are the natural fits for the **before/after Match Score visual**. Ad 2 pairs with a side-by-side "ChatGPT output vs. ResumeTailor output."
- **Retargeting:** Ads 10 and 12 work best for `score_preview_view` abandoners — price reassurance + full-value recap.
- Keep primary text front-loaded — Meta truncates after ~125 characters before "See more."
