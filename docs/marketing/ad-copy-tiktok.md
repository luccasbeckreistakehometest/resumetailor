# ResumeTailor — TikTok UGC Scripts

> 8 short-form, UGC-style hooks with 3–15s scripts, each in **EN / pt-BR / ES**, with on-screen text suggestions.
> Style: handheld, talking-to-camera or screen-record demo, native and unpolished. Avoid "ad voice." Show the product (the paste → Match Score → finished resume flow) wherever possible — the demo IS the proof.
>
> **Compliance:** no "get hired" guarantees, no fake numbers. The Match Score shown on screen is a real product output on the creator's own resume (e.g. "38% → 91%" must reflect an actual run). Disclose paid partnerships per TikTok rules. Keep captions readable (3–5 words per line, high contrast).
>
> **Format tips:** hook in the first 1.5s, on-screen text reinforces audio, end on a clear verbal + text CTA. Trending audio low under voiceover. 9:16, 15–30s total works best even though scripts focus on the core 3–15s beat.

### Production & testing notes
- **Best performers to shoot first:** Hook 2 (screen demo — the score climbing is the single most persuasive moment) and Hook 1 (ChatGPT contrast — the platform-native objection).
- **Spark Ads:** if a creator's organic post performs, boost it as a Spark Ad rather than re-uploading — keeps native engagement signals.
- **Variation:** film 2–3 hook variants per script; TikTok creative fatigues fast — plan a fresh batch every 1–2 weeks.
- **Truthfulness:** any on-screen "38% → 91%" must be a real run from the creator's own resume. Vary the numbers across creators so it reads as authentic, not a scripted claim.
- **Captions/audio:** assume sound-off viewing first 2s; the hook must land in on-screen text alone.
