# ResumeTailor — Campaign Plan v2 (loss-aversion + value)

> One-time **$9 USD / R$39 BRL** per tailoring. No subscription. On-demand.
> Paste a job description + your resume → in ~30s get a **tailored resume +
> matching cover letter + LinkedIn "About"**, with a quantified **Match Score**,
> ATS-safe formatting, and two finished formats (ATS/digital + designed PDF).

This is a sharpening of `campaign-plan.md`, not a replacement. v1's funnel,
budget split, KPIs, naming convention, and 7-day launch still stand. v2 changes
the **angle**: from "stop guessing" to **loss aversion + visible value** —
*you are losing the job you want by not tailoring* — kept strictly honest.

---

## 1. The core shift: loss aversion + value

People act harder to **avoid a loss** than to chase a gain. The job seeker's loss
is concrete and emotional: the role they actually want, going to someone whose
resume simply *matched the posting better*. v1 sold relief from uncertainty ("stop
guessing"). v2 sells **the cost of inaction** — then immediately shows the
**value** that removes it (Match Score + ATS + the 3-piece kit).

**The master angle (one sentence):**
> "You're not losing jobs because you're unqualified — you're losing them because
> your resume doesn't match the posting. Tailor it in 30 seconds and see your
> Match Score before you pay."

Why this is honest:
- It blames the **document-to-posting mismatch**, which is real and fixable — not
  the person, and not a guaranteed outcome.
- "You're losing jobs" is framed as a **risk of the generic-resume status quo**,
  supported by cited industry data (62% of managers more likely to reject a
  generic/un-customized resume; ~98% screen with an ATS; ~7-second scan). It is
  NOT "you WILL get this job if you buy."
- The payoff is a **tool output you can see for free** (the Match Score), not a
  promise. Loss aversion creates the pull; the free preview proves the value.

**Two-beat structure for every ad:** `[LOSS] → [VALUE/PROOF]`.
1. **Loss beat:** name the silent cost (ghosted, beaten by a worse candidate who
   matched better, your resume dies in the ATS scan).
2. **Value beat:** the Match Score you see free, the keyword gaps named, the
   finished kit, $9 one-time.

## 2. Honesty guardrails (apply to ALL copy — unchanged, reinforced)

- **No** "get hired," guaranteed-interview, or guaranteed-job claims. Loss aversion
  must target the **generic-resume status quo**, never imply our tool guarantees
  the win. Allowed: "you're losing jobs to a resume that doesn't match." Banned:
  "don't lose this job — buy and you'll get it."
- **No invented statistics.** Only cite figures from `market-data-2026.md`, with
  the source + year shown. Never the debunked "75% rejected by ATS" myth (or its
  pt-BR "ATS filtra 75% dos currículos" / ES variants).
- **Match Score** is a real product output describing *the user's own document vs.
  the job's keywords*. Frame as "see your Match Score," never as a hiring-outcome
  promise.
- **Defensible verbs:** tailor, match, optimize, highlight, format, see, improve,
  build, fix the gap. **Banned:** guarantee, ensure, land, win, beat the bots
  (in a promise sense).
- Price/time claims ("~30 seconds," "$9 / R$39") must stay accurate to live product.
- **Weaving stats in (the rule):** a stat is *industry context for the loss*, never
  our result. Pattern: "[cited industry stat] — that's why a generic resume loses.
  Tailor yours and see the Match Score." Always show "Source, YEAR" on-creative or
  in the caption. Every `[STAT: ...]` placeholder in `ad-copy-v2.md` resolves to a
  numbered entry here / in `market-data-2026.md`.

## 3. Positioning vs. the alternatives (the value the loss angle pays off)

The loss is real **because the easy alternatives don't fix the mismatch:**

| Alternative the seeker uses now | Why it still loses the job | Our value beat |
|---|---|---|
| Same resume to every posting | Doesn't match *this* job's keywords; dies in the ~7-sec scan / ATS rank | Per-job keyword gap + rewrite to match |
| Paste into ChatGPT | Generic text, bad formatting, no idea if it's good — and managers reject generic AI output | Recruiter/ATS expertise baked in + Match Score that proves it + 2 finished formats |
| Pay a $200 resume writer | Slow, expensive, one static version | $9, ~30s, re-tailor per job, no subscription |
| Do nothing / apply less | The role goes to whoever matched better | See the gap free; close it for $9 |

**The value is shown, not asserted:** the **before/after Match Score** ("matched
38% of this job's keywords → 91%") is the hero asset. It is the visible proof that
the loss is real *and* solvable.

## 4. Per-niche strategy & strongest angle

Run EN (international), pt-BR (Brazil), ES (LATAM) as **separate ad sets/campaigns**
so CPMs, price points, and CPA targets don't blend. (Budget split, naming, and

### Global EN — strongest angle: "Beaten by a better-matched resume"
- **Loss:** "Someone less qualified than you got the interview — because their
  resume matched the posting and yours didn't." Plays on competence anxiety in a
  flooded market.
- **Value:** Match Score before/after + named missing keywords + the 3-piece kit,

  `[STAT: 62% of managers more likely to reject a generic/un-customized resume —

### Global / LATAM ES — strongest angle: "Tu CV genérico es por qué no te llaman"
- **Loss:** "No es que no estés calificado — es que tu CV no coincide con la
  vacante, y por eso no te llaman." Direct, status-quo-cost framing.
- **Value:** "Mira tu Match Score gratis, corrige lo que falta, llévate CV + carta
  + LinkedIn. Pago único, sin suscripción."
- **Proof to weave:** `[STAT: ~98% ATS — Jobscan 2025]`,
  `[STAT: 62% rechazo de CV genérico — Resume Now 2025]`. Keep price framing
  flexible ("pago único / desde $9") since price varies by country.
- **Channel lean:** Meta + TikTok where native ES UGC exists; Google Search on

### Brazil pt-BR — strongest angle: "Seu currículo morre na triagem da Gupy"
- **Loss:** "A Gupy lê seu currículo antes de qualquer recrutador. Currículo
  genérico, sem as palavras-chave da vaga = você fica para trás." Hyper-specific

- **Gupy angle — honesty:** Gupy/Gaia genuinely ranks candidates 0–100 on keyword
  fit; per Gupy's own data, most hires came from the AI's top recommendations

  guarantee approval, and **never** use the "Gupy filtra 75%" myth.
- **Channel lean:** Meta + TikTok (native pt-BR UGC, "como passar na Gupy" is a
  huge search/UGC theme) + Google Search ("currículo para Gupy," "currículo ATS").

## 5. Funnel (ad → /lp or /start → free Match-Score preview → paywall)

Funnel mechanics, tracking event names, and budget logic are unchanged from v1.
v2 only adds **how the loss/value beats map onto each step:**

```
COLD AD  →  /lp  (cold, quiz: "What's your situation?")
[LOSS hook]   or  /start  (intent, direct paste box)
   │              headline mirrors the niche loss angle above
   ▼
PASTE JD + RESUME  ("no resume?" → guided build flow)
   │  user invests effort → loss feels personal & concrete
   ▼
FREE MATCH-SCORE PREVIEW   ← the value beat, free
   │  show CURRENT score + top missing keywords (the visible "loss")
   │  blur/lock the rewritten doc + improved score
   │  CTA: "Close the gap — unlock your tailored kit, $9"
   ▼
$9 / R$39 PAYWALL  → ATS/digital + designed PDF + cover letter + LinkedIn About
                     (Pix for BR; Apple/Google Pay + card)
```

- **/lp** = cold, loss-led, quiz personalizes the headline by situation
  (applying now / career change / first job / laid off).
- **/start** = high-intent (Google), direct paste box above the fold, minimal copy.
- **The preview IS the value proof.** Showing the current low score + named gaps
  makes the loss tangible *and* the $9 fix obvious. This is where loss aversion
  converts: the user has now *seen* what they're losing.
- **Capture email at preview** for retargeting the abandoners (cheapest conversion).

**Tracking events (unchanged):**
`lp_view → quiz_complete → input_submit → score_preview_view → checkout_start → purchase`.
Optimize toward `score_preview_view` early, shift to `purchase` at ~30–50 conv/wk.

## 6. KPIs / targets

Carry over v1's benchmark table (CTR/CPC/LP→preview/preview→purchase/CAC/ROAS by
channel) unchanged. v2 adds angle-test KPIs so we learn whether loss aversion
actually beats the v1 "stop guessing" angle:

- **Primary A/B:** v2 loss angle vs v1 "stop guessing" angle, same creative format,
  per market. Win metric: **cost per `score_preview_view`** first, then **CVR
  preview→purchase** and **CAC vs $9**.
- **Hook-rate (Meta/TikTok):** 3-sec video views / impressions — does the loss
  hook stop the scroll harder? Target the loss hook to beat control by ≥15%.
- **Preview→purchase by niche:** expect Brazil/Gupy and Google high-intent to lead.
- **Guardrail metric:** comment sentiment / report rate — loss aversion can read as
  fear-mongering if overdone. If negative sentiment or "misleading" reports spike,
  soften the loss beat (it must stay status-quo-cost, never doom).
- **North star (weekly, per market):** blended **ROAS** and **CAC vs $9**.
