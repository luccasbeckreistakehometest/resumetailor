# ResumeTailor — Google Search (Responsive Search Ads)

> Per language: **15 headlines** (≤30 chars) + **4 descriptions** (≤90 chars), plus keyword themes, match-type guidance, and negatives.
> RSA best practice: pin sparingly (pin 1–2 brand/price assets to position 1 only if needed), let Google rotate the rest. Aim for "Excellent" ad strength by keeping headlines varied (benefit, feature, price, CTA).
>
> **Compliance:** no hiring guarantees, no fake stats. "Match Score" = a feature the user sees on their own resume. Price tokens accurate: $9 (EN), R$39 (BR), "pago único" (ES, price varies by country).

## Match-Type Guidance

| Stage | Match type | Why |
|-------|-----------|-----|
| **Launch (Themes 1, 2)** | **Phrase + Exact** | Highest intent. Tight control while budget is small — don't pay to learn on junk traffic. |
| **Themes 3, 5, 6** | **Phrase** | Captures intent variations without going fully broad. |
| **Scale phase** | **Broad + Smart Bidding (tCPA/tROAS)** | Only after you have ~30+ conversions feeding the algorithm. Always pair broad with a strong negative list and watch the search-terms report weekly. |
| **Theme 4 (no experience)** | **Phrase**, lower bids | High volume, lower commercial intent (many are students researching) — qualify carefully, expect lower CVR. |

**Rules**
- Start with Exact + Phrase only. Add Broad after week 2 once conversion data exists.
- Bid highest on Theme 1 (closest to purchase), lowest on Themes 4/5.
- Mine the search-terms report every few days early on; promote good queries to Exact, add bad ones to negatives.
- Use ad-group-level negatives to keep "first CV" queries out of the "tailor to job" group and vice versa.

## Negative Keyword List

Add as campaign-level negatives (phrase/broad) unless noted. Localize for BR/ES.

**Free / no-pay intent**
- `free`, `free resume`, `free resume builder`, `100% free`, `no cost`, `gratis`, `grátis`, `gratuito`, `de graça`

**Templates / DIY only (low CVR for a paid tool)**
- `template`, `templates`, `word template`, `google docs template`, `modelo de currículo word`, `plantilla cv word`

**Jobs/research, not resume tools**
- `jobs`, `job openings`, `vacancies`, `hiring near me`, `vagas de emprego`, `ofertas de trabajo`, `empleos`

**Salary / career research**
- `salary`, `how much does`, `salário`, `salario`, `cuánto gana`

**Education / how-to-only (when targeting buyers)**
- `course`, `class`, `tutorial`, `pdf download`, `examples` (test — some "examples" buyers convert; monitor), `curso`, `exemplos`, `ejemplos`

**Unrelated meanings of "resume"**
- `resume meaning`, `resume definition`, `resume playback`, `resume download` (software), `currículo lattes` (BR academic system — different product), `curriculum vitae meaning`

**Adjacent products we don't sell**
- `linkedin premium`, `interview questions`, `mock interview`, `job board`, `recruiter contact`

**Wrong audience**
- `for hiring managers`, `applicant tracking system software` (B2B buyers of ATS, not seekers), `ats software pricing`

> Note: keep `cheap` / `barato` OUT of negatives — at $9 these are buying signals. Review the search-terms report weekly and grow this list; a tight negative list is the single biggest CAC lever on Search for a low-ticket product.

## Ad Group Structure (recommended)

One campaign per market (`Google_EN_Search`, `Google_BR_Search`, `Google_ES_Search`), each with ad groups mapped to Themes 1–6. Give Themes 1 & 2 the most budget and the tightest match types. Point all RSAs at the **direct paste landing page** (no quiz) for Search — intent is already high, minimize friction to the Match-Score preview.
